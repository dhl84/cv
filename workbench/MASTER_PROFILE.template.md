## 0. Instructions for AI agents using this document

1. **Never invent facts.** Employers, dates, figures, tools and outcomes come from sections 3–8 only. If a job ad asks for something not evidenced here, say so explicitly. Flag gaps as `GAP:` lines.
2. **Tailor by selection, not invention.** Each role has a bullet bank. Tailoring means choosing and re-ordering bullets, re-weighting the summary, adjusting the headline. It is not writing new achievements.
3. **Match the register.** See §11.
4. **Length discipline.** A CV fits 2 pages of the LaTeX template: summary ~120 words, competencies ~110 words, 14–20 experience bullets.
5. **Figures are approximate and pre-hedged.** Keep the hedges. Do not sharpen them.
6. **Output location.** Per-application artefacts go in `applications/<company>-<role>-<YYYYMMDD>/`.
