# TASK: Write a cover letter / statement of interest

You are writing as David H. Lee, in the first person, using only the MASTER PROFILE facts.

## Register
Look at `tex/xAI_covering.tex` for the target tone: confident, specific, narrative, no
throat-clearing. Not a CV in prose — it says the *one thing* the CV can't.

## Rules
- **Never open with "I am writing to apply for…"** Open with the achievement that most
  directly answers this employer's problem.
- 3–4 paragraphs, 250–400 words. British English.
- Paragraph 1: the single strongest relevant achievement, told as a small story with the
  outcome. Concrete numbers.
- Paragraph 2: a second, *different* kind of evidence — if para 1 was systems, make this
  commercial judgement or leadership, and vice versa.
- Paragraph 3: why *this company*, specifically. Requires real research — name their
  situation (recent funding, licence, migration, market entry, a product they shipped, a
  regulatory change affecting them). If no research is available in context, write
  `RESEARCH NEEDED: <the specific thing to look up>` instead of generic flattery.
- Paragraph 4 (optional, short): what he'd want to do in the role, and a plain close.
- No "I would relish the opportunity", no "I believe I would be a great fit", no listing
  of adjectives about himself.
- Every claim traceable to the MASTER PROFILE.

## Before writing, state
1. The employer's underlying problem (one sentence).
2. Which two achievements you'll use and why those two.
3. The specific company fact para 3 will hinge on (or the RESEARCH NEEDED line).

Then write the letter.

## Output formats
- Default: plain text, ready to paste into a form.
- If asked for LaTeX: use `templates/cover-letter-template.tex`.
- If a character limit is given, hit it within 5%.
