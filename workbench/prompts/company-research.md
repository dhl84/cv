# TASK: Research a company before applying or interviewing

Produce a research brief David can read in 10 minutes before a call.

## What to find
1. **The business.** What they sell, to whom, how they make money, revenue/headcount/stage,
   who owns them (listed / PE / VC — and which investors, last round, valuation).
2. **The finance context.** Latest filed accounts (Companies House for UK entities), revenue
   trend, profitability, going-concern or audit commentary, auditor identity and any
   qualification, group structure and material subsidiaries. For regulated firms: FCA
   register status, permissions, safeguarding arrangements, any enforcement.
3. **Recent news, last 12 months.** Funding, M&A, layoffs, leadership changes, product
   launches, market entry/exit, regulatory actions, outages, lawsuits.
4. **The finance function.** Who is CFO, how long, where from. Recent finance hires on
   LinkedIn. Is the team growing? Is there a visible transformation programme (ERP
   migration, IPO prep, licence application)?
5. **Why this role exists now.** Backfill, growth, or a problem? Evidence for your answer.
6. **Culture and process.** Interview process per Glassdoor/Blind, values, working pattern,
   comp benchmarks for the level and location.
7. **The interviewers**, if named: current role, tenure, prior employers, education,
   published content (talks, posts, papers), plausible shared ground with David.

## Rules
- **Cite a source for every factual claim** — URL or publication. Mark anything inferred as
  `INFERENCE:`. Mark anything you could not verify as `UNVERIFIED:`.
- Do not guess financials. If accounts aren't available, say so.
- Do not speculate about named individuals beyond their public professional record.
- Prefer primary sources (company site, Companies House, FCA register, filings, press
  releases) over aggregators.

## Output
```
## Snapshot (5 bullets: what they do, size, stage, owner, why interesting)
## Business model & how money flows
## Financials & audit
## Last 12 months — timeline of notable events
## Finance function & likely pain points
## Why this role exists (with reasoning)
## Interviewers
## Interview process & comp expectation
## Three things David can say that show he did the work
## Five questions David should ask them
## Sources
```
